How I work

A short, measurable process: understand first, then propose, and only then invest in technology.

  1. Diagnosis

    Interviews with the people who do the work, a review of data and systems, and a baseline of indicators.

  2. Proposal

    A prioritized improvement plan, with the recommended technology solution for each item and its estimated effort.

  3. Implementation

    I support execution with your team or vendors, caring for the change in people and not only in tools.

  4. Measurement

    We compare against the baseline and I leave the team able to sustain the improvement on its own.
