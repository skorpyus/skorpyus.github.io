Consulting for SMEs

Less manual work, more time to grow your business.

I help small and medium-sized businesses improve their day-to-day processes: organizing them, redefining roles and controls, and automating or digitizing what makes sense. The goal is simple: fewer repetitive tasks, fewer errors and more productivity.

Does any of this sound familiar?

These are signs that some processes are costing your company more time and money than they should.

  • The same data is entered several times, in different spreadsheets.
  • You don't know exactly how much stock you have until someone counts it.
  • Orders and deliveries are tracked over WhatsApp, email or from memory.
  • Purchases and approvals depend on one person being available.
  • Closing the month, running checks or preparing an audit takes days.
  • Errors show up late, after they've already hit a customer or cash flow.

Areas I work on

  • Sales and quotes
  • Purchasing and supply
  • Stock and inventory
  • Logistics and deliveries
  • Administration
  • Controls and audit

The solution isn't always technology. Sometimes it's enough to organize the process, change the methodology or redefine roles and controls; when it adds value, I complement that with automation, systems integration, AI or custom development, sized to each company's budget.

What your company gains

  • More productivity. Your team stops doing repetitive tasks and focuses on selling, serving customers and growing.
  • Fewer errors. Data is entered once and controls apply automatically.
  • Information to decide. Sales, stock, purchasing and deliveries up to date, in one place.
  • Control and traceability. Every transaction is recorded, with clear owners by role.
  • Processes that don't depend on one person. The workflow keeps running when someone is out or leaves.
  • Growth without extra admin load. More transactions with the same structure.

How I work

First understand how your company works today, then propose the changes, and only then add technology if needed.

  1. Diagnosis

    I walk through the processes with the people who run them, spot manual tasks, duplication and risks, and measure how they work today.

  2. Proposal

    A plan prioritized by impact and effort, with the recommended solution for each process and its estimated cost.

  3. Implementation

    I support the rollout with your team or vendors, including training the people who will use it.

  4. Measurement

    We compare against the starting point and I leave the team able to sustain the improvement on its own.
