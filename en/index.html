About me

I lead engineering teams that build digital products for financial services and insurance. At itti I work on the products that made Ueno Seguros the first insurer in Paraguay to offer fully online management, from sale to after-sales.

I started as a programmer and have been leading teams since 2008 at Tarshop, OCA, BNP Paribas Cardif, Supervielle Seguros, Santander Tecnología and itti. In the last two, I grew the teams (up to 10 engineers), designed and drove their split into two squads to give each roadmap a vertical focus, and supported the growth of the people who lead them.

I don't code day to day: I lead the teams that build. I read and interpret code, with AI support, to diagnose incidents, contribute to solution design and propose improvements in efficiency, technical debt, scalability and architecture.

Three ideas that guide how I lead

Data-driven decisions.

I cross-reference Jira and GitHub metrics (cycle time, time to first review, throughput) to find bottlenecks, and I use a high-level estimation model so product and business can prioritize the roadmap with more certainty.

AI applied to the development lifecycle.

I drove an AI-assisted SDLC and Spec-Driven Development in my team, with quality and security built in from design.

People first.

I work with the talent team on hiring engineers, support each person's growth with clear goals and promote those who are ready to lead.
